A footnote has a reference and a definition.[^1] The **Footnote** command picks the next free number and leaves the cursor in the definition.[^2]

An inline note keeps its text right where it is used.^[Inline notes need no label.]

Labels belong to their file, so another piece can also use `[^1]` without a clash.

[^1]: The definition sits at the end of the piece.
[^2]: In the preview, click a note number to jump to the note, and the ↩ mark to jump back.
