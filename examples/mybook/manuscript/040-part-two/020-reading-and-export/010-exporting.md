The **File** menu exports the book four ways:

- **Export Manuscript** writes one Pandoc Markdown file.
- **Export PDF** lays out the whole book. Front matter and each chapter print their own notes.[^1]
- **Export Chapters to Word** writes one `.docx` per front-matter piece and per chapter.
- Right-click a chapter and choose **Export to PDF** for that chapter alone.

[^1]: So this note prints at the end of chapter 5.
